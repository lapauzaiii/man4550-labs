The Analyst Challenge: Audit [#]

[Decision Model Topic]

Executive Scenario

Client division: [Cowford division or business unit]

Decision under review: [Briefly describe the management science decision.]

Report source: The executive report you are reviewing was produced with AI assistance and sent to Cowford Advisory Group for verification before leadership presentation.

Your Task

Review the executive report and determine whether the analysis deserves to be trusted. You are not simply checking arithmetic. You are evaluating the model, assumptions, interpretation, and recommendation.

Required Workflow

  1. Read the Executive Report.
  2. Verify the quantitative model.
  3. Correct any errors.
  4. Classify each finding as Critical, Major, or Minor.
  5. Complete the Confidence Statement.
  6. Write a one-paragraph Executive Brief.
  7. Complete the AI Disclosure.

Submission Checklist

  • Cover sheet with audit identification and recommendation status
  • Audit findings table
  • Corrected calculations, Solver output, sensitivity interpretation, or other model evidence as appropriate
  • Confidence statement
  • Executive brief, written as one paragraph with no equations
  • AI disclosure statement

Executive Brief Requirement

Your executive brief must be one paragraph. It should state whether the original recommendation should be accepted, revised, or rejected; identify the most important reason; and explain the business consequence in professional language.

Grading Emphasis

You will be evaluated on quantitative verification, quality of corrections, severity classification, managerial interpretation, professional communication, and responsible AI disclosure.
