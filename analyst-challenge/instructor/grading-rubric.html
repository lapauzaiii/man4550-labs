Grading Rubric

Suggested total: 100 points. Adjust point values for shorter audits or weekly assignments.

CriterionPointsExcellentDevelopingInsufficient
Model Verification25Accurately checks variables, objective, constraints, formulas, and reported results.Checks most model components but misses one important verification step.Relies mainly on the report without meaningful independent verification.
Error Correction20Corrects errors clearly and supports revisions with appropriate quantitative evidence.Finds some errors but corrections are incomplete or weakly documented.Corrections are missing, unsupported, or mathematically incorrect.
Severity Classification15Classifies findings as Critical, Major, or Minor with sound reasoning tied to decision impact.Uses severity labels but reasoning is uneven or too calculation-focused.Labels are missing or do not reflect business consequence.
Managerial Interpretation15Explains how corrected results affect feasibility, resource allocation, profitability, risk, or recommendation quality.Provides some interpretation but does not fully connect model results to management action.Focuses on numbers without explaining the decision consequence.
Executive Communication15Writes a concise, polished paragraph appropriate for leadership.Message is understandable but too technical, too long, or not fully executive-facing.Brief is unclear, missing, or written as disconnected bullets.
Confidence and AI Disclosure10States confidence honestly, identifies uncertainty, and discloses AI use responsibly.Includes the required elements but with limited reflection.Disclosure or confidence statement is missing or perfunctory.
